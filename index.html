1 · SHOW HOW SURE IT IS

Every field says how the agent got it. Read from the photo, matched to the card charge, or a guess. Only the guesses need the person's attention, so only the guesses ask for it.

2 · THE QUIET MISTAKES ARE THE BAD ONES

In Gauravide a £20 receipt once reached Splitwise as $20, with no error shown. A wrong currency looks just like a right one. So the agent checks it against something real, the card charge, and asks when the two don't line up.

3 · EVERY STEP CAN BE UNDONE

The agent lists what it did, and each line has its own undo. Finance sees the same list, so nobody has to guess why an expense passed or got flagged.

A concept by Gaurav Basantani for the Senior Product Designer, Travel & Expenses role. Not made with or for Brex, and no Brex data or brand is used. The receipt, merchant and numbers are made up; the exchange rate is a round example.
